Accepted

HVGuard: Utilizing Multimodal Large Language Models for Hateful Video Detection
EMNLP 2025 Main Conference · First Author
A multimodal hateful-video detection framework that uses multimodal large language models, chain-of-thought reasoning, and mixture-of-experts fusion to better capture cross-modal interactions and implicit hateful content.
Q-Regularized Generative Auto-Bidding: From Suboptimal Trajectories to Optimal Policies
KDD 2026 Main Conference · First Author
A generative auto-bidding method that incorporates Q-value regularization into a Decision Transformer framework to learn better policies from suboptimal historical trajectories and guide action exploration.
Generative Auto-Bidding with Unified Modeling and Exploration
SIGIR 2026 Oral · First Author
A unified generative auto-bidding framework combining Decision Transformer, inverse dynamics modeling, and Q-value-based action selection to balance exploration quality and safety.
